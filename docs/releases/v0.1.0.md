A native Windows version of *Valkyria Chronicles III* for PSP, built on your PC from your own disc image. This download contains no game data.

**Beta:** this first release has been tested through Chapter 1.

### You need

- Your own disc image (`.iso`) of Valkyria Chronicles III (Japan v1.01, ULJM-05781) with the English fan-translation patch applied. Unpatched Japanese discs aren't supported yet.
- The free **Visual Studio 2022 Build Tools** with "Desktop development with C++", which compiles the game on your PC.

### How to play

1. Download `VC3Recompiled-v0.1.0-Windows-x64.zip` below and unzip it.
2. Run `VC3Launcher.exe`, pick your ISO, then press **Recompile** (about 2 minutes) and **Play**.

Or install it through [RecompLauncher](https://recomplauncher.com), which uses the included `recomplauncher.json`.

### What works

- Boot, menus, the full Chapter 1 story, the story map, and the first battle (tactical map and 3D action).
- Movies with sound, music and voices.
- Saving from the in-game menu and loading from the title screen.

### PC features

- Up to 8× resolution, MSAA, FXAA, anisotropic filtering and texture upscaling.
- Windowed, borderless or fullscreen.
- Keyboard and controller support.
- An optional 60 FPS mode for battles (experimental).

### Known limitations

- Later chapters are not tested yet.
- Saves only work with this port, not with a real PSP.
- No local multiplayer.

The launcher's About tab shows this build's version and commit. Use "Copy build information" when you report a problem.
